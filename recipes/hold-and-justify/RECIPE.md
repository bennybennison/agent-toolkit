# Hold And Justify

Use this recipe when the safest action is to avoid unnecessary work, ask for clarification, or explicitly confirm that the current state is already acceptable.

The orchestrator should:

1. Inspect only enough context to justify the decision.
2. Explain why a stronger recipe is not currently needed.
3. Capture the next best action, unanswered question, or reason no action is needed.

Do not silently continue into build or repair work from this route.
