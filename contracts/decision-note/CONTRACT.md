# Contract: DecisionNote

## Purpose

`DecisionNote` records why no immediate implementation or broader workflow was
chosen, and what should happen next instead.

## When To Use

Use `DecisionNote` for:

- `hold-and-justify`
- status-only decisions
- clarify-before-action outcomes

## Required Sections

- current request
- decision
- why no stronger action is needed
- next action or clarification needed
