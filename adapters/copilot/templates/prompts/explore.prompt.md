Explore the following rough idea without jumping straight to implementation.

Produce an `IdeaBrief` that includes:

- the core idea
- who it helps
- key use cases
- open questions
- risks and edge cases
- alternatives considered
- recommendation:
  - proceed to mapping
  - proceed to feature spec
  - defer
  - reject for now

Use a lightweight discovery mindset. Do not over-specify the solution too early.
