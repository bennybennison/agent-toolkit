Diagnose and repair the following bug or unstable behavior.

Follow this order:

1. Reproduce or define the reproduction blocker
2. Produce a `BugDiagnosis`
3. Rank the most likely causes
4. Choose the repair strategy
5. Propose or implement the narrowest credible fix
6. Define focused verification evidence

Do not jump from symptom to fix without naming the diagnosis clearly.
