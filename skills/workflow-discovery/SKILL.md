# Skill: Workflow Discovery

Understand the user workflow before designing screens or backend systems.

## Goal

Produce a `WorkflowBrief` that explains:

- who the user is
- what job they need to complete
- what decisions they make
- what blockers they face
- what success looks like

## Questions To Answer

1. Who is the primary user?
2. What triggers this workflow?
3. What are the main steps they take?
4. Where do they currently lose time or confidence?
5. What information do they need at each step?
6. What exceptions or failure cases appear in real work?

## Output Shape

Capture:

- user roles
- core jobs
- step-by-step workflow
- pain points
- decision points
- success criteria

Do not drift into implementation detail yet.

## Anti-Patterns

- Jumping to tables or endpoints before the user job is clear
- Describing screens before the workflow is understood
- Writing generic personas with no operational value
